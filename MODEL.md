# Model definitions and remaining assumptions

## Accounting

Each path maintains separate cash, taxable investments and basis, retirement accounts, loans, a home and mortgage, outstanding tax settlement and unmet bills. Household net worth is financial net worth plus home equity. The separately reported business interest is illiquid and excluded from household net worth. Liquid wealth subtracts outstanding taxes and unmet bills from cash and taxable investments.

Every month checks the balance-sheet change against income, expenses, investment gains, interest, employer retirement contributions and home appreciation. A discrepancy over five cents raises an error. Marginal medians are never treated as a reconciled individual ledger.

Shared costs follow available income before marriage. Personal spending retains ownership. Spendable cash can transfer between partners after marriage. Unmarried Amanda with no income is assigned zero shared costs while her own personal expenses continue against her assets. With two zero incomes, neither household cash nor borrowing capacity is invented. Optional deferrals stop when required costs are unaffordable. Cash is used first, then taxable assets; remaining costs become unpaid obligations. Tax settlement occurs in April, with an annual December liability reconciliation. Monthly withholding is an estimate and can differ from annual taxes.

The loan engine uses simple accrued interest during deferment and capitalizes it when repayment starts, then amortizes the outstanding balance. Subsidized loans accrue no interest during configured deferment. Required repayment starts in the first full month after the configured date. Extra payments follow the configurable interest hurdle and transition band. Alternative income-driven repayment rules, forgiveness and borrower-specific recertification are not implemented.

The household saving target reserves $2,000 from monthly surplus before additional debt/investment allocation. It is not a guarantee that cash grows by $2,000 in a deficit month. Amanda keeps a six-month baseline spending reserve. Existing cash interest and investment returns continue after an employment stop. Investment returns are a single portfolio return process, not a security-level trade simulator.

## Families, work and housing

Events occur at the configured month within each selected year, except the February 2027 UD/DARPA transition and configured grant dates. Stop-at-birth policies remain inactive if that birth never occurs. Paid childcare is zero for every child when Amanda stops. Otherwise school-age care defaults to 40% of preschool care and can be edited. Healthcare and other child costs continue. Leave defaults to three months at half Amanda's pay and is distinct from permanently stopping work.

Logan's eligible family coverage is used first after Amanda stops. Unmarried partner eligibility is false by default; replacement adult coverage is added in that case. With no Logan coverage, both adults and all children receive explicit replacement premiums. The family premium and medical out-of-pocket amounts are provisional averages. The lost-UD-benefits branch removes medical coverage and employer retirement contributions; voluntary employee deferrals are provisionally assumed available and can be disabled through the contribution-rate input. Plan-specific deductibles, claim-level expenses, ACA subsidies, domestic-partner imputed income and exact enrollment/COBRA dates are not implemented.

Faculty salary anchors are academic-year salaries. Configured summer months and support fractions determine the annual compensation, spread over 12 months. Industry and faculty concurrent-company cases are conditional on employer permission and capacity, not assertions that any employer permits them. Offer probabilities, job-search duration, Amanda's regional career factors and faculty salaries are estimates. Employer compensation ranges do not establish a probability distribution over individual offers.

Housing purchases wait until down payment, closing costs, reserves and the configured DTI test are affordable. Prices follow the same simulated housing index before and after purchase. The model charges property tax, insurance, maintenance, PMI and mortgage interest/principal. It currently has one move and one possible purchase per scenario; resale, refinance and repeated moves are not implemented. Terminal home equity is market value minus debt, without hypothetical selling costs.

## Company, grants and equity

Grants finance company expenses and never enter household cash directly. Cost-based grants reimburse actual eligible spending, subject to the award cap, available effort, cost sharing and delays. Total claims cannot exceed company spending. Milestone advances remain restricted until eligible costs occur. Employee/cofounder capacity and incremental staffing are explicit assumptions. Unpaid founder compensation is tracked; this is not a full corporate accounts-payable or GAAP revenue-recognition system. Unused award budget is not assumed to have been paid.

The DARPA case is approximately $2 million over 24 months, not $2 million of household income or a guaranteed lump-sum receipt. Cost reimbursements can total less than the award when modeled eligible spending is lower. The proposed split is $31,500 UD plus up to $82,500 founder compensation from February 2027, subject to company liquidity. Award failure preserves full UD compensation in the blended path. Delayed funding can reduce actually paid founder compensation. Other grants and commercial follow-ons remain separate conditional or sampled outcomes. Foundation and joint-research categories are illustrative structures, not confirmed awards or eligibility determinations.

The provisional cap table applies original founder ownership, then the Braydon issuance, then Diane's issuance. Subsequent financing and pool increases dilute earlier holders. Stephen's contractual participation is a claim against the residual common proceeds rather than another founder equity percentage. Monthly timing approximates the end of his seven-year participation period. Relevant vesting is assumed complete. Confirm exact agreement interpretation before relying on an exit estimate.

The waterfall pays transaction costs and senior debt, then honors investor liquidation preferences or conversion elections. It supports nonparticipating and participating preferred stock, subsequent rounds and simplified SAFE/note conversion assumptions. Instruments that have not converted at exit are treated as debt principal; negotiated liquidity-event multipliers and note-specific provisions need separate configuration/implementation. Option pool changes are fractional dilution assumptions, not fully solved negotiated pre-money pool targets.

Enterprise value and company cash are reconciled to distributable proceeds. Corporate asset sales receive a provisional 26% corporate tax charge before shareholder distributions. Escrow and earnouts delay household receipts and recover basis proportionally. Earnouts can fail. The basis input is an initial estimate; partnership basis adjustments, installment-sale tax elections, contingent-payment rules, depreciation recapture and full asset allocation are not modeled. Buyer-funded employment compensation continues after an exit at the modeled pre-exit salary, conditional on retained employment; other chosen careers also continue.

## Tax scope

The engine includes projected 2026 federal brackets and standard deductions, ordinary and long-term capital-gain stacking, child and dependent-care credits, Social Security, Medicare, self-employment tax, Additional Medicare, NIIT, retirement deferral limits and conditional QSBS. The dependent-care credit uses the enacted 2026 two-stage phase-down. SALT has its scheduled 2030 return to $10,000. Future indexed thresholds are estimates; statutory fixed thresholds stay fixed where implemented.

QSBS is off by default. Original issuance, conversion timing, holding period, company qualification, basis and state conformity require confirmation. The helper represents post-July 2025 holding-period tiers and the $15 million/ten-times-basis cap; it is not an eligibility determination. State conformity flags are editable hypotheses.

State calculations for PA, DE, ME, NY, CA and MA are simplified schedules. Interstate taxes allocate annual income by residence/source weights and apply a simplified resident credit. They do not reproduce all state tax forms, NY recapture, local schedules, deductions or benefit rules. Delaware remote-source share is explicit and unresolved. CA payroll disability and corporate state taxes are provisional.

Material exclusions include federal AMT, QBI deduction, earned-income credit, head-of-household and alternative dependent allocation, dependent-care FSA elections, capital-loss carryforwards, lot-specific holding periods, state short-term gains differentiation, full mortgage-interest limitations, ownership-specific investment tax allocation before marriage, and legal partnership basis adjustments. Retained partnership profit is provisionally ordinary pass-through income; its active/passive employment-tax treatment needs refinement. Therefore reported exit taxes are conditional planning estimates, not accurate tax-return or exact take-home calculations.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Historical calibration preserves monthly dependence across inflation, equity, home prices and cash yields using contiguous block resampling. The downloaded history has 115 aligned months, ending June 2026; two months were excluded because a missing CPI value also prevents the next month's return calculation. There is no interpolation across this gap. The dataset is recent and includes a strong equity-market period, so its empirical return distribution should be stress-tested against alternative assumptions and longer historical series. The dividend proxy and national-to-local housing extrapolation are explicit limitations.

Parametric mode uses correlated Student-t innovations. Macro offsets are structural scenarios, including AI transitions; GDP narratives are not converted mechanically to equity returns. Wage/return relationships and business shock dependencies are assumptions. At present inflation uses a common household index rather than separately calibrated category-specific processes. Full unemployment/revenue contagion calibration remains incomplete.

A 95% predictive interval means the model's 2.5th through 97.5th percentiles conditional on the inputs. It is not a 95% guarantee or a calibrated confidence interval for unknown housing or salary assumptions. Exact binomial intervals quantify Monte Carlo uncertainty in shortfall rates; mean standard errors are separate. Tail warnings remain on until at least 20 expected samples populate each 2.5% tail. Sensitivity reruns are needed for parameter and structural uncertainty.

The practical schemas intentionally reduce combinations. The full grid preserves exhaustive declared event combinations, with inactive dimensions collapsed where implemented. Annual birth schedules use distinct years with a twelve-month minimum; same-year twins and finer within-year birth-grid expansion are not implemented. The app and report announce their catalog/plot subsets. A weighted forecast uses retained path samples and explicitly subjective weights, not averages of conditional scenario percentiles. The current forecast additionally conditions on the SaaS operating case with no VC funding or exit; it is not an unconditional company-success forecast.

## Further work for the original completion criteria

The implemented workflow runs, reconciles, saves, resumes, reports and supports selected reruns through configuration/CLI. The simplified dashboard is an output explorer. Remaining work includes geography-specific wage/housing/childcare calibration; tax exclusions listed above; category-specific inflation; additional event types; more detailed grant/contract budgets and corporate accounting; full household GPU implementation/parity; formal convergence and calibration studies; and expanded causal sensitivity/exit-waterfall report designs. These are visible limitations rather than implied completed features.

## Retirement readiness

This is a diagnostic attached to every stochastic household path. It does not stop employment or rewrite the ten-year ledger. Both adults are assumed to retire together; age is reported for Logan using his age at the September 2026 starting balances. Checks occur at December year ends, so fractional ages are approximate given the unknown birth date.

Accessible capital starts with liquid wealth less outstanding nonmortgage debt and a flat reserve on positive taxable gains. Retirement balances receive a provisional 20% tax haircut. Home equity and unsold business interests are excluded. Lifetime required capital is the greater of 25 times annual spending (the configurable 4% withdrawal cap) and the stochastic lifetime withdrawal requirement. Money accessible before the younger adult reaches 59.5 must independently cover the intervening withdrawal years; no early-withdrawal exception is presumed.

Annual real withdrawals occur at the beginning of each retirement year through the younger adult's age 100. Common draws use lognormal annual real growth with 3% arithmetic expected return and 15% volatility, with 4,096 retirement paths by default. Required capital is the corresponding discounted withdrawal annuity. Both the lifetime and access-bridge requirements use the 97.5th percentile for a 95% combined success target via a conservative union bound. This is an empirical model target with finite Monte Carlo error, not a guaranteed success rate or a calibrated safe-withdrawal rule. The 4% cap can bind even for short retirements.

The `lifecycle_v2` budget separates ongoing adult costs from dated obligations. Ordinary child support runs to age 18; college replaces that support from ages 18 through 21 and all support ends at 22, with partial calendar years respected. Parents pay half of an editable $28,500 annual public in-state college cost in 2026 dollars. The amount is a rounded projection from [NCES institutional cost components](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/search/viewtable?returnUrl=/search&tableId=36306), not a school quote, enrollment-weighted average, or assumed financial-aid award. Paid childcare ends at 13, and is zero when Amanda stays home.

Existing mortgages amortize from each path's actual purchase date and ending balance for the configured term, 30 years by default with 20 and 25 also supported. Fixed nominal payments shrink in real purchasing power using the editable 2.5% post-horizon inflation assumption. PMI ends with the modeled loan-to-value threshold. Property tax, insurance and maintenance continue after payoff; renters continue paying their real rent. Home size and ongoing real housing costs are held constant after the horizon, without automatic downsizing, sale or an invented later purchase. An unaffordable purchase still leaves the path renting.

The ongoing retirement portfolio budget is adult living costs, continuing rent or owner expenses and $18,000 annual retirement healthcare, with a $50,000 adult spending floor. Finite obligations, including children, college, the remaining mortgage and known future purchase/wedding/move costs, are reserved separately at **zero real return**. Lifetime capital is that reserve plus the ongoing budget's stochastic capital requirement; the accessible-account bridge includes temporary payments due before account access. This explicit conservative reserve avoids treating temporary bills as perpetual withdrawals or assuming market gains will pay them. Planned obligations therefore do not disappear upon early retirement. Set `annual_spending_override` only to deliberately replace the complete retirement budget, including these obligations. Medical and withdrawal-tax assumptions remain provisional.

After the ten-year horizon, spendable resources are estimated from the median of the last three annual real saving-plus-spending amounts, adjusted for one-time costs and debt payments already reserved in accessible assets. Net brokerage flows include withdrawals and exclude market returns; positive contributions alone would miss deficit spending. Future contributions subtract each year's dated expenses from those resources, increasing when children leave or mortgages end and decreasing during college. Retirement saving additions retain the recent contribution pattern. New reproducible stochastic real returns are shared across scenarios. This remains a conditional accumulation projection, not a full post-2036 career, benefit and tax ledger: Social Security, pensions, new jobs/exits/grants, healthcare eligibility changes and detailed withdrawal sequencing are not added. Long-term investment assumptions are separate from the ten-year macro narratives.

Every terminal path contains the first qualifying age and a source of `within_simulation`, `beyond_horizon_projection`, or `not_reached`. Evaluation stops at the configured maximum retirement age, 80 by default. Unreached values remain explicit and are treated as infinity when calculating scenario age quantiles. Missing summary quantiles mean that quantile was not attained by the maximum age, rather than an average computed only over successful paths. Readiness probabilities across household paths and the assumed conditional retirement success target describe different uncertainties.

## 2026 purchasing power and compensation

Monetary bands and summary outcome statistics are in 2026 purchasing power. The cumulative inflation index starts at September 2026 balances and advances from October. Each path is deflated before computing percentiles or summing real flows. Raw terminal and monthly accounting ledgers retain nominal amounts; terminal `real_*` columns expose corresponding purchasing power. Report workbooks and plotted ledgers use deflated dollars. Scenario input fields such as contractual exit value and grant amount remain nominal event assumptions, not outcomes.

Industry salary ranges are user assumptions, sampled with a common triangular quantile across comparable careers: bio-AI $200k/$250k/$600k (minimum/mode/maximum), pharma/agritech $100k/$300k/$500k. A new external role indexes its starting offer from 2026 dollars to the entry month. Annual review dates apply accumulated nominal wage shocks; compensation is flat between reviews except modeled leave, gaps or role changes. Amanda has her own review clock. Founder pay increases annually while employed, including retained employment after exit. Fixed grant caps are not increased to cover inflation. Existing faculty/UD inputs remain distinct from the new landscape distributions.

New default scenarios omit UD nonrenewal and assign zero involuntary UD job-loss probability. Other role disruptions remain configurable. Career, location, career-change year and move year are independent. Unusual combinations remain conditional on employer availability/permission, which the model does not establish.

## Automatic decision analysis

Cash zero means end-of-month cash at most one cent. Liquid depletion means cash plus taxable investments less outstanding tax liabilities and unpaid bills at most one cent. Distress is either liquid depletion or an unmet bill. A net-worth reversal requires a previous positive simulated monthly net worth before a nonpositive value; a path that merely starts with student debt is not classified as a reversal. No within-month liquidity ordering beyond the monthly engine is inferred.

The first depletion date is retained per path, along with whether it precedes the new career and whether it falls within 12 months after a scheduled birth, move or Amanda leaving work. Event shares use depleted paths as their denominator. Overlapping events can both be counted; these are associations, not causal attribution.

Salary frontiers preserve common-random-number pairing. For each career family, location/macro scope and path index, distress is ORed over the tested scenario combinations. The resulting observations are independent path draws, not scenario-path rows. Within each $50k band, the event frequency estimates risk under that band's conditional salary distribution and all choices in that scope. It does not guarantee each individual salary point. One-sided exact binomial upper limits use a Bonferroni correction across every displayed cell, including location-specific and all-location/all-macro screens. Thresholds require at least the configured number of paths and all higher tested bands to satisfy the risk budget. No monotonicity is assumed, and no extrapolation fills empty bins.

The all-choice event is more conservative than any one scenario's marginal failure probability. Its pointwise conclusions remain conditional on all cost, tax, career and macro assumptions. New calibration or parameter uncertainty can change them. A missing supported threshold is explicitly reported rather than implying impossibility or safety. The analysis includes pre-job failures: a high eventual salary cannot retroactively pay earlier unmet bills.

All risk CSVs cover the completed report catalog. Excel sheets retain at most 10,000 rows, explicitly labeled in report scope; Parquet and CSV retain complete results. Distribution plots use up to 1,000 evenly spaced catalog scenarios and explicitly do not represent a calibrated forecast.
